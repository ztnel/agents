Keys

j k · 5j · gg G · 42G
Move to the character above or below on screen, keeping the column; a diagram is one stop (h/l step into its text). Counts repeat. NG jumps to source line N.
Ctrl-d Ctrl-u · Ctrl-f Ctrl-b · zz
Half page / full page down and up; centre the cursor.
[[ ]]
Previous / next heading.
h l · w b e · 0 ^ $
Move by character, word and to the line start, first character or end. Works inside diagram text and math; on a diagram without text h/l cycle its labelled elements.
v · V · o
Enter VISUAL (characters) or VISUAL LINE and extend with motions; o swaps the selection end.
c · C
Comment on the cursor line, selection or diagram element · on the whole document.
r · Enter
Reply to the conversation on the cursor line · show it in the panel.
m M
Next / previous conversation.
Tab
Open the floating conversation panel, then switch focus between it and the document; Esc closes the panel and returns to the document. In the panel: j/k move, r reply, Enter jump to its text. (Ctrl-w is not bound: browsers close the tab.)
/ · n N · :noh
Search (smartcase) any text, diagram labels and math included, landing on the exact character; next/previous match; clear highlights.
Enter · Shift+Enter · Esc
In INSERT: send; newline; close the conversation keeping the draft.
:w · :q · :wq · :N
Save as PDF via the print dialog · end the session · both · go to source line N.
Click · Shift+click
Move the cursor and open the conversation on highlighted text · extend a character selection.
Esc · ?
Clear count, selection or element · this help.
